One topic per line, a few words each. Posts about anything else get blurred out. You can always click a blurred post to read it.

How to write a good topic
  • Two to five words. One word is too vague. More than five and each one counts for less.
  • Keywords, not sentences. Every word counts, so small words like and or posts about only blur what you're asking for.
  • Commas don't matter. software, programming and software programming work the same. Use whichever you prefer.
  • One subject per line. Put a separate subject on its own line. A post that matches any line stays visible.
  • Use the words posts use. software works better than tech, because people write about the thing, not the category.
  • It matches subjects, not quality. A great post and a poor one about the same thing both stay. FeedLens can't tell them apart.

Details

FeedLens only understands English. It can't tell whether a post in another language is about your topics or not, so it lets some through and blurs others at random. Turn this on to blur them all — including ones that are on topic.

Details

Hover over a post to give it a thumb up or down.

  • 👍 means this content belongs in the topic.
  • 👎 means this content does not belong in the topic.
  • A correct call — blurred or shown — needs no correction.

A thumb only changes posts that are almost the same as the one you rated, such as a repost or a quote. Everything else is judged by your topics as usual, so a thumb never hides a good post by accident.

Your ratings stay on this device. One catch: it only compares the words in a post. If the real content is in the image or video and the words are forgettable, skip it. Leave this off and the buttons stay hidden.
