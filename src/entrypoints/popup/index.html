How to write a good topic
  • Two to five words works best. One word is vague; past five, each extra word dilutes the rest.
  • No sentences, no filler. Every word is matched, so and, posts about and the drag the topic toward nothing in particular.
  • Commas are optional. software, programming and software programming score the same. Use whichever reads better.
  • One topic per line. Words on the same line describe one subject; a new line is a separate subject, and a post matching any line stays.
  • Use the words posts actually use. software beats tech, because people write about code, not about categories.
  • Subject, not quality. A shallow take and a deep post on the same subject both match. Lensing cannot tell them apart.
